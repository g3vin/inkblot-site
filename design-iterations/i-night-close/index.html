Part two · The marketplace

Read each other.

Real readers. Not a model. Post a chapter, a story or a script, and other writers review it, line by line.

  1. 01

    Read

    Take on someone's chapter.

  2. 02

    Review

    Notes in the margin, a report at the end.

  3. 03

    Earn

    Coins for every finished review.

  4. 04

    Be read

    Spend them to post your own.

  • Coins can't be bought. Every review is earned.
  • Take on a work and you finish it.
  • Every copy carries its reader's watermark.
  • Report a reviewer who isn't human.

The world is yours. So is every word.

Nothing in Inkblot writes for you. Your work stays on your device unless you choose to share it, and it never trains anything.

A few small models run on your device for jobs that aren't writing: synonyms, dictation, reading aloud. None of them adds a word.
