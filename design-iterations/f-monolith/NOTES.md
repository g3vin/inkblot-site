# F · Monolith on location

**Concept.** The dailies' favourite ("Monolith on location", loved), built as a whole
page:
- Ink (dark) by default.
- A poster hero sized to the screen, with "Write." at the foot, one line and one button
  on a rule, and the app window's edge just below the fold.
- Full-bleed place bands, each carrying one huge line: "Then it becomes a book." and
  "The world is yours. So is every word."
- A numbered, giant roster.
- The six stops as page-shaped slots.
- The same terse copy as E.

**Placeholders.** The two place bands reuse the leaves still for now. Each is tagged with
the photograph it's waiting for: harbour at dawn, remote cabin.

**Contrast.**
- Ink theme: text 15.9:1, muted 7.0:1, Fern 8.2:1.
- Paper-white over the darkened bands measures about 12:1 at the foot, where the text
  sits.

**Lighthouse (mobile).**
- Scores: Performance 98, Accessibility 100, Best practices 100, SEO 100.
- LCP 2.3 s, TBT 0 ms, CLS 0, 206 KB before the video.

**axe** (Paper, Ink, Black at 1280 and 375): 0 violations.

**Trade-offs.**
- The loudest and darkest option, and the most dependent on real photography. It needs
  at least three strong, vivid place pictures in one grade.
- It sells mood over features.
