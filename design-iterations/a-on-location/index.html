Print

Print-ready, not just printable.

Choose a trim size and a book style, and Inkblot sets every page: chapter openings, running heads, folios, the contents. The cover gets a spine and bleed, and it's checked the way KDP and IngramSpark check it.

For the book you write after everyone's asleep.

Pitch black turns the screen's pixels off and sets your words in a warm ember. Easy on the eyes at 2 a.m., and on OLED screens it uses almost no light at all.

Our pledge

Inkblot never writes for you.

There's no generative AI in Inkblot: nothing that drafts, rewrites, continues, summarises or "improves" your words, and no chat. Your manuscript stays on your device as ordinary files. We never see it, and it will never be used to train anything.

A few small models run on your device for jobs that aren't writing: finding synonyms and rhymes, turning your voice into text when you dictate, and reading your words aloud. They work offline, they never send your text anywhere, and you can write without them.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in Inkblot today stays free. There may be optional extras later, but nothing you rely on now will move behind them.

Which devices does it run on?

Mac, Windows and Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android.

Does Inkblot use AI?

Nothing in Inkblot writes, rewrites, continues or summarises your words. A few small models run on your device for synonyms and rhymes, dictation and reading aloud. Your manuscript is never used to train anything.

Where are my files, and can I take them with me?

On your device, as ordinary files in a folder you choose. There's no account to close. Export to PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or HTML whenever you like; canvases are JSON Canvas files that also open in Obsidian.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Writing, planning, typesetting and export all work with no connection. A few extras, like satellite map imagery and dictionary definitions, go online only when you ask for them.

Is the PDF ready for KDP and IngramSpark?

Inkblot exports the interior at your trim size and the cover as one full-wrap PDF with ⅛ in bleed, and checks the cover as KDP and IngramSpark would: 300 dpi pictures, a spine sized to your page count, no spine text under 80 pages. Always order a proof copy before you publish.

How is it different from Scrivener, Vellum or Atticus?

Scrivener is made for drafting and organising; Vellum and Atticus for formatting. Inkblot does both, adds maps, timelines and canvases for the world behind the book, and it's free, works offline and needs no account.
