What it is

One window. Every draft.

Inkblot is a place to write a book from the first sentence to the printed page. You draft in a quiet editor, prose or screenplay, and speak a passage if your hands are tired. The world sits beside it: boards for ideas, maps of the places, a timeline of who was where.

When the draft is done, you don't export it to another program. You switch to Typeset and the chapters become pages: a book style, a trim size, chapter openings, a cover with a spine. Then a print PDF, an EPUB and a Word file, from the same book.

It never writes a word for you. It never sees a word you write.

Everything lives on your device, as ordinary files in a folder you choose. There's no account, no sync we run, and no tracking. It works offline, and it's free.

Our pledge

Inkblot never writes for you.

There's no generative AI in Inkblot: nothing that drafts, rewrites, continues, summarises or "improves" your words, and no chat. Your manuscript stays on your device. We never see it, and it will never be used to train anything.

A few small models run on your device for jobs that aren't writing: synonyms and rhymes, dictation, and reading aloud. They work offline and never send your text anywhere.

Questions

What is Pitch black?

A theme that turns the background fully off and sets your words in a warm ember instead of white. Turn it on with "Pitch black when dark" in Settings; Paper and Ink are there for the day.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in Inkblot today stays free. There may be optional extras later, but nothing you rely on now will move behind them.

Does Inkblot use AI?

Nothing in Inkblot writes, rewrites, continues or summarises your words. A few small models run on your device for synonyms and rhymes, dictation and reading aloud. Your manuscript is never used to train anything.

Which devices does it run on?

Mac, Windows and Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android.

Where are my files?

On your device, as ordinary files in a folder you choose. Export to PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or HTML whenever you like.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Writing, planning, typesetting and export all work with no connection.

How is it different from Scrivener, Vellum or Atticus?

Scrivener is made for drafting and organising; Vellum and Atticus for formatting. Inkblot does both, adds maps and timelines, and it's free, offline and needs no account.
