# B · The proof

**Concept.** Print first. The hero picture is the book itself: a 6×9 spread at true
proportion inside crop marks. The page follows the book being made:
- six page-shaped steps from idea to proof copy;
- the seven book styles as typeset chapter heads;
- a printer's spec sheet (trim, cover, checks, export, history);
- only then the worldbuilding tools.

The leaves appear once, as a still behind the final "Write.".

**Target emotion.** Confidence: "this will actually produce a book I'm proud of."

**Sub-line tested:** "From first sentence to print-ready book, in one free app. Offline,
and every word your own." (audit option 1, adapted for every platform).

**Type.**
- Inter Display Bold for headlines (−6.5% on "Write.").
- Literata for reading text.
- Courier Prime for folios, spec labels and figure captions.
- The style cards use Literata italic and small caps, Inter, and Courier to suggest each
  style. They're labelled as illustrations, to be replaced by real exports.

**Palette and contrast.** The same tokens as A: ink on paper 16.0, muted 5.5 (5.1 on
the sunk panels), Fern 9.4, white on Fern 10.0, and in the Ink theme 15.9 / 7.0. Pitch
black is available through the theme switch: Ember 8.9, muted 5.8. The only colour
beyond ink and paper is Fern, on buttons and the Modern style's numeral.

**Trade-offs.**
- It answers "does it do print?" fastest, and Vellum and Atticus users will recognise
  the vocabulary straight away.
- It leans on assets that don't exist yet: real spreads, a real cover, real exports of
  all seven styles. With placeholders it looks the most unfinished of the four.
- It's the least atmospheric. The leaves are reduced to a closing band.
- It risks reading as a formatting tool and hiding the maps and timeline.

**Who it would appeal to most:** self-publishers, and anyone comparing against Vellum or
Atticus.
**Who it might lose:** first-timers and worldbuilders, for whom print feels far away.

**Lighthouse (mobile, simulated slow 4G, uncompressed local server).**
- Scores: Performance 99, Accessibility 100, Best practices 100, SEO 100.
- LCP 2.2 s (the "Write." headline), TBT 0 ms, CLS 0, FCP 1.4 s, 193 KB.

**axe** (Paper, Ink, Black at 1280 and 375): 0 violations.
