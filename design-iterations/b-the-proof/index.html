Our pledge

Inkblot never writes for you.

What it will never do

There's no generative AI in Inkblot: nothing that drafts, rewrites, continues, summarises or "improves" your words, and no chat. Your manuscript stays on your device as ordinary files. We never see it, and it will never be used to train anything.

What runs on your device

A few small models do jobs that aren't writing: finding synonyms and rhymes, turning your voice into text when you dictate, and reading your words aloud. They work offline, never send your text anywhere, and you can write without them.

Questions

Is the PDF ready for KDP and IngramSpark?

Inkblot exports the interior at your trim size and the cover as one full-wrap PDF with ⅛ in bleed, and checks the cover as KDP and IngramSpark would: 300 dpi pictures, a spine sized to your page count, no spine text under 80 pages. Always order a proof copy before you publish.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in Inkblot today stays free. There may be optional extras later, but nothing you rely on now will move behind them.

How is it different from Vellum or Atticus?

They format a finished manuscript. Inkblot is where you write it too, with maps, timelines and canvases for the world behind it, and it's free, works offline and needs no account.

Does Inkblot use AI?

Nothing in Inkblot writes, rewrites, continues or summarises your words. A few small models run on your device for synonyms and rhymes, dictation and reading aloud. Your manuscript is never used to train anything.

Which devices does it run on?

Mac, Windows and Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android.

Where are my files, and can I take them with me?

On your device, as ordinary files in a folder you choose. Export to PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or HTML whenever you like.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Writing, typesetting and export all work with no connection. A few extras, like satellite map imagery and dictionary definitions, go online only when you ask for them.
