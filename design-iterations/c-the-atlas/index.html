The book

Then it becomes a book.

Typeset mode shows every chapter as real pages. Pick one of seven book styles, set a trim size, add front and back matter, and export a print PDF and a cover with spine and bleed, checked the way KDP and IngramSpark check it. The EPUB comes from the same pages.

Our pledge

The world is yours. So is every word.

There's no generative AI in Inkblot: nothing that drafts, rewrites, continues, summarises or "improves" your words, no invented names or places, and no chat. Your manuscript and your maps stay on your device as ordinary files. We never see them, and they will never be used to train anything.

A few small models run on your device for jobs that aren't writing: finding synonyms and rhymes, turning your voice into text when you dictate, and reading your words aloud. They work offline and never send your text anywhere.

Questions

Do I need to be online to use maps?

No. Worlds you draw, planets and galaxies, and the Earth's coastlines and borders all work offline. Satellite pictures, street detail and place search are optional and off until you turn them on.

How is it different from World Anvil or Campfire?

Inkblot keeps the world beside the manuscript, then typesets the book. It's an app on your device, not a website: free, offline, and no account.

How is it different from Scrivener, Vellum or Atticus?

Scrivener is made for drafting and organising; Vellum and Atticus for formatting. Inkblot does both, plus maps, timelines and canvases.

Does Inkblot use AI?

Nothing in Inkblot writes, rewrites, continues or summarises your words, or invents names and places. A few small models run on your device for synonyms and rhymes, dictation and reading aloud. Your work is never used to train anything.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in Inkblot today stays free. There may be optional extras later, but nothing you rely on now will move behind them.

Which devices does it run on?

Mac, Windows and Linux, iPhone, iPad and Android.

Where are my files?

On your device, as ordinary files in a folder you choose. Export to PDF, EPUB, Word, Markdown or HTML whenever you like.
